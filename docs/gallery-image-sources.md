## Rights status — action required before production

published by Mujaddidun to its own Nahno NGO profile and campaign pages, so the association is the
likely rights holder.

before the site goes live publicly. Queen Rania’s official Flickr photographs of the same visit are
marked All Rights Reserved and were deliberately **not** used.

## Deleted albums — "فعاليات الإفطار الخيري" and "حملة التشجير"

Both seeded albums were removed on 2026-08-17 after an exhaustive source search found no
photograph that could be verifiably attributed to a Mujaddidun activity of that kind.

**حملة التشجير (tree planting).** Mujaddidun's own campaign listing on Nahno contains 81
campaigns; **none** is a tree-planting or environmental campaign. The listing breaks down as
Ramadan parcel distribution (54), دفء blanket distribution (19) and أضاحي meat distribution (8).
Every tree-planting campaign surfaced by search belongs to a *different* organisation — Yarmouk
University, the Balqa Governorate leadership programme, "من أجل الأردن للتدريب", the Crown Prince
Foundation Ma'an office and مبادرة سلام — so their photographs were excluded.

**فعاليات الإفطار الخيري (charity iftar).** Iftar *is* a genuine Mujaddidun activity — the
association runs إفطارات الأيتام under its نُطعِم programme and the "خيمة الفرح" Ramadan tent — but
no photograph of it is reachable on any accessible public source. The only Mujaddidun-specific
media source that covers خيمة الفرح is the Al Jazeera feature, whose four images are two
food-parcel photographs (already used elsewhere on this site) and two individual portraits — no
iftar scene. Other iftar photographs found belong to other charities (جمعية جبيهة الخيرية and
others) and were excluded. Mujaddidun's own photo archive lives on Facebook/Instagram, which are
login-walled.

Before deletion, every file belonging to the two albums was confirmed to be referenced only by
those albums (a full scan of all 30 database tables), so nothing else on the site was affected.
Deleted rows and files are backed up outside the repository.

Removed files: `gallery-covers/album-1-cover.jpg`, `gallery-images/sample-0.jpg` … `sample-5.jpg`.

Re-confirmed on 2026-08-17: a second, wider sweep — all 84 distinct scenes from Mujaddidun’s own
campaign photo set, plus fresh searches of Petra, Al-Anbat, Ammon and Al Jazeera — produced no iftar
and no tree-planting photograph. Textual confirmation that the iftar activity is real does exist
(the Orange partnership article cites "خيم الإفطار الرمضانية للأيتام"), but no photograph of it is
published anywhere reachable.

If the association supplies its own iftar or environmental photographs, either album can be
recreated from the backup.
